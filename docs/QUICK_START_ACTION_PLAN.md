## Week 4: Close (Days 22-30)

### Focus: Convert Pilots & Close Deals

**Activities**:
1. **Pilot check-ins**: Daily support for active pilots
2. **Proposal negotiations**: 3-5 proposals in flight
3. **Case study development**: Document pilot successes
4. **Referral requests**: Ask happy beta users for intros

### Pricing Negotiation Guide

**If they say "Too expensive"**:
- Pivot to Starter tier ($3K vs $8K)
- Offer payment plan (quarterly)
- Emphasize ROI: "One job placement tracked = $8K value"
- Propose success-based pricing: "$500 per successful placement"

**If they say "We need more time"**:
- Extend pilot (free additional 30 days)
- Offer phased rollout (start with Directory only)
- Get commitment: "What needs to happen in the next 30 days for you to move forward?"

**If they say "We prefer [competitor]"**:
- Use `COMPETITIVE_BATTLECARD.md`
- Focus on speed: "2 weeks vs 3 months implementation"
- Focus on specialization: "Maritime-specific vs generic"
- Focus on cost: "1/3 the price for 90% of the features you need"
