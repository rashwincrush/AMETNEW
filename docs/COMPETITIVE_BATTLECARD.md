## Competitor 1: Almabase

### Overview
- **Type**: Generic alumni engagement platform
- **Target**: All universities (not industry-specific)
- **Pricing**: $8,000-$25,000/year
- **Customers**: 300+ institutions globally

### Their Strengths
✅ Established brand name  
✅ Wide feature set (giving, events, email, jobs)  
✅ Strong in US higher ed market  
✅ Good customer support reputation  

### Their Weaknesses
❌ **Not maritime-specific** - generic one-size-fits-all  
❌ **Expensive** - 2-3x our pricing  
❌ **Complex implementation** - 8-12 weeks typical  
❌ **Limited job matching** - no education-based algorithms  
❌ **Add-on pricing** - mentorship, advanced features cost extra  

### How to Win
**Angle**: Maritime specialization + speed + value

**Key Questions to Ask Prospect**:
1. "Does Almabase understand the difference between a marine engineer and a naval architect?"
2. "What's your timeline? Almabase implementations typically take 3 months. We can have you live in 2-4 weeks."
3. "Have you seen their mentorship module? It costs extra and isn't as structured as ours."

**Proof Points**:
- "We match jobs to degrees automatically - Almabase requires manual tagging"
- "Our customers save $10K+ annually vs Almabase pricing"
- "We handle 5 user roles natively - Almabase requires custom configuration"

**When to Walk Away**:  
If prospect says "We need every feature Almabase has" and budget isn't a constraint. They're looking for a different solution.

- **Pricing**: $10,000-$30,000/year
- **Customers**: Large research universities

✅ Established in UK/EU markets

❌ **No mentorship module** - networking only

**Key Questions**:
1. "Are you looking for just a directory, or a full career development platform?"
2. "How important is mentorship to your institution? Graduway doesn't have structured mentoring."
3. "Do you need job matching based on education? Graduway is keyword search only."

**Proof Points**:
- "We have a full mentorship program with capacity management - Graduway has no equivalent"
- "Our job portal matches by degree automatically"
- "Career services teams prefer our application tracking"

## Competitor 3: LinkedIn Groups / Pages

### Overview
- **Type**: Social media platform
- **Cost**: Free
- **Usage**: Most alumni already on LinkedIn

### Their Strengths
✅ Users already there (no adoption friction)  
✅ Professional network  
✅ Free to create groups  
✅ Good for broad awareness  

### Their Weaknesses
❌ **No institutional control** - LinkedIn owns everything  
❌ **No structured features** - no events, mentorship, job tracking  
❌ **Algorithm limits reach** - posts seen by <5% of members  
❌ **Unprofessional noise** - mixed with personal content  
❌ **No data ownership** - can't export or analyze  
❌ **No privacy** - public by default  

### How to Win
**Angle**: Professional, owned, controlled, measurable

**Key Questions**:
1. "Who owns the data in your LinkedIn group? Hint: not you."
2. "Can you track how many alumni found jobs through LinkedIn?"
3. "Do you have moderation tools? Can you remove inappropriate content quickly?"
4. "How many of your posts actually reach alumni? LinkedIn algorithms throttle reach."

**Proof Points**:
- "You own 100% of your data on our platform - export anytime"
- "Our messaging is connection-gated for safety - LinkedIn is open to anyone"
- "We provide full analytics - LinkedIn gives you almost nothing"
- "Our job matching is automated - LinkedIn requires manual searching"

**Common Objection**: "But alumni are already on LinkedIn"  
**Response**: "Exactly - they're already there. That makes LinkedIn great for awareness, but terrible for structured engagement. You can't run a mentorship program in a LinkedIn group. You can't track job placements. You can't control the experience. Think of LinkedIn as the billboard, and our platform as your alumni center."

## Competitor 4: WhatsApp Communities / Groups

### Overview
- **Type**: Messaging app
- **Cost**: Free
- **Usage**: Very popular in India/Southeast Asia

### Their Strengths
✅ High engagement (messages get read)  
✅ Everyone has it on phone  
✅ Free  

### Their Weaknesses
❌ **Chaotic** - no structure, impossible to find information  
❌ **No career tools** - just chat  
❌ **No admin controls** - anyone can message everyone  
❌ **Unprofessional** - personal and professional mixed  
❌ **No analytics** - zero insights  
❌ **Privacy concerns** - phone numbers exposed  
❌ **Scalability issues** - groups limited to 1,024 members  

### How to Win
**Angle**: Professional structure vs chat chaos

**Key Questions**:
1. "How do you find a job posted 2 weeks ago in a WhatsApp group?"
2. "Can you moderate content? What happens when someone posts something inappropriate?"
3. "How do you track event RSVPs or collect feedback?"
4. "Are alumni comfortable sharing their phone numbers with 5,000 people?"

**Proof Points**:
- "We have structured modules - Jobs, Events, Mentorship - not just chat"
- "Our directory is searchable by degree, year, location - WhatsApp has no directory"
- "Connection-gated messaging prevents spam - WhatsApp is open season"
- "Full analytics on engagement - WhatsApp gives you nothing"

## When to Compete vs. When to Walk Away

### ✅ Pursue Aggressively
- Prospect is unhappy with current generic platform
- Timeline is urgent (< 3 months to launch)
- Budget is constrained
- Maritime-specific features are important
- Career services is a priority

### ⚠️ Qualify Carefully
- Prospect is evaluating multiple options (ask: what are top 3 priorities?)
- Large institution with complex requirements
- Needs extensive integrations

### ❌ Walk Away (Politely)
- "We just need a simple directory" → WhatsApp/LinkedIn might be fine
- "Budget is unlimited and we want every feature" → Almabase might be better fit
- "We're 2 years away from any decision" → Set reminder, stay in touch lightly

## Post-Loss Analysis

**Always ask after losing a deal**:
1. What was the primary reason we weren't selected?
2. What could we have done differently?
3. Would you consider us for future expansion/additional modules?
4. Can we stay in touch for feedback?

**Document and share** these insights monthly with product and marketing teams.
