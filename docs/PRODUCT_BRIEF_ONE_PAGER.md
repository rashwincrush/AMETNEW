# Forgecircle Alumni Platform - Product Brief (One-Pager)

**For**: Maritime Education Institutions | **Date**: April 2026 | **Version**: 1.0

---

## The Problem

Maritime universities struggle with alumni engagement:
- 📉 **Disengaged Networks**: Alumni lose touch within 2 years of graduation
- 📊 **Manual Processes**: Excel spreadsheets, email chains, no tracking
- 💼 **Career Gap**: No structured way to connect students with industry
- 🎯 **No Insights**: Zero visibility into alumni career paths

**The Cost**: Lost donation opportunities, weak employer partnerships, declining institutional reputation.

---

## The Solution

**Forgecircle Alumni Platform (A3)** — The only purpose-built alumni engagement platform for maritime education.

Built for the unique needs of marine engineers, naval architects, and port operations professionals.

---

## Core Capabilities

### 🎓 For Alumni
- **Directory**: Find classmates by graduation year, degree, department, location
- **Jobs**: Education-matched opportunities from verified maritime employers
- **Mentorship**: Structured mentor matching with capacity management
- **Events**: Reunions, webinars, networking - all in one place
- **Groups**: Join alumni communities (by region, industry, graduation year)

### 🏢 For Institutions
- **Admin Console**: Role-based access, audit logs, user management
- **Career Services**: Job posting, application tracking, employer management
- **Event Management**: RSVP, reminders, feedback collection
- **Analytics**: Engagement metrics, career tracking, reports

### 🤝 For Employers
- **Verified Talent Pool**: Access to confirmed maritime graduates
- **Targeted Posting**: Reach candidates by degree and specialization
- **Application Management**: Track, communicate, hire efficiently
