## Methodology

1. **College Selection:** Compiled from Wikipedia lists of engineering colleges and institutions of higher education in Tamil Nadu
2. **Website Discovery:** Used known domain patterns and official college websites
3. **Content Analysis:** Automated scanning for alumni-related keywords (alumni, alumnus, alumna, graduates, alumni association, alumni network, alumni portal, alumni directory, alumni chapter, alumni meet, alumni registration, alumni login)
4. **Link Detection:** Identified navigation links and pages related to alumni activities
5. **Classification:** Categorized colleges based on presence/absence of alumni platforms

---

## Limitations

1. **Website Accessibility:** 36 colleges had inaccessible websites due to DNS failures, SSL errors, or timeouts
2. **Keyword-Based Detection:** May miss alumni platforms using non-standard terminology
3. **Single-Page Scanning:** Only homepage was scanned; alumni content may be on sub-pages not linked from homepage
4. **Dynamic Content:** JavaScript-heavy sites may not have been fully analyzed
5. **Language Barriers:** Alumni content in regional languages may not have been detected

---

## Next Steps

1. **Manual Verification:** Contact colleges with inaccessible websites to verify alumni platform status
2. **Deep Scanning:** Implement multi-page crawling for colleges with accessible websites
3. **Regional Language Support:** Add Tamil language keyword detection
4. **Outreach Campaign:** Begin systematic outreach to target colleges
5. **Follow-up Analysis:** Track responses and engagement rates
