## Email Tips & Best Practices

### Subject Line Formulas That Work
1. **Question format**: "Quick question about {{specific thing}}"
2. **Personalized**: "Saw {{Institution Name}} just launched {{program}}"
3. **Curiosity gap**: "The #1 mistake maritime universities make with alumni"
4. **Social proof**: "How {{similar institution}} increased event attendance 3x"
5. **Direct**: "Demo: Forgecircle Alumni Platform for {{Institution Name}}"

### Timing
- **Best days**: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- **Best times**: 8-10 AM, 2-4 PM (prospect's local time)
- **Follow-up cadence**: Day 3, Day 7, Day 14, Day 28, then monthly

### Personalization Checklist
- [ ] Mention institution name in first sentence
- [ ] Reference recent news/achievement if available
- [ ] Use their specific job title
- [ ] Include relevant case study (similar size/region)
- [ ] Tailor CTA to their role (career services vs alumni relations)

### Metrics to Track
- Open rate target: >40%
- Reply rate target: >10%
- Meeting booked rate: >5%
- Unsubscribe rate: <2%
